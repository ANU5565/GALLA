# GALLA-
“Galla” is the kirana owner’s cash drawer, so the name tells the story at once.

Most teams will pitch “AI inventory plus demand forecast”. GALLA treats this as a working-capital problem. Dead stock is cash that stopped moving, and a stockout is cash missing where it’s needed. GALLA is an agent that watches the shop, proposes the best use of the owner’s cash budget each day, prepares orders for distributors, and sends nothing without the owner’s tap. It learns from every correction. Neighbouring shops can pool orders and swap near-expiry stock. The anonymised demand signal is valuable to distributors and brands, and that pays for the product.
